Who runs this place

The meowner

Dawn Loehlein — the shop’s self-styled “meowner” and “opurrator” — worked as a manager at Hot Topic for many years before opening Neon Kitten on Broadway.

That résumé is the shop, inside out: a love of cats, a punk rock education, and stock that leans weird on purpose.

The Columbian profiled the new store on November 10, 2026: “A shop with cattitude.”
