What's in the Shop

Four lanes, one shop on Broadway. Neon Kitten carries cat-themed merchandise, punk-flavored goods, and vintage and secondhand finds — plus a deep bench of things that don't fit anywhere else.

Lane 01

Cat-themed gifts

Cat stuff with cattitude. The gift side of the shop is cat everything through a punk filter — picked by a self-styled “meowner” who stocks the cat things she actually wants to buy.

Gift hunting for a specific cat-obsessed human? See the cat-gifts lane or email the shop.

Lane 02

Punk & alternative

The punk lane comes from experience: owner Dawn Loehlein worked as a manager at Hot Topic for many years before opening Neon Kitten. The shelves keep that instinct — goods with a stance.

Details on the punk & vintage page.

Lane 03

Vintage & secondhand

Pre-loved pieces with history attached. The Columbian's write-up of the opening names this lane as the heart of the deal: “like the vintage and secondhand things.”

One piece, one copy. When it's gone, it's gone — that's the point.

Lane 04

One-of-a-kind weirdness

The fourth lane has no clean name because it can't be pinned down: “unique, weird, one-of-a-kind things — the weirder, the better,” as the shop's coverage put it. This is where you find it.

Stock moves and nothing is listed online, so the best move is to come look — or ask about something specific.
