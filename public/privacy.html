The small print

Privacy

What this site collects

This site sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics and no tracking scripts. Pages are plain files served as they are.

The embedded map gets its tiles from google.com, and Google may set its own cookies for that map — see Google's own privacy policy for that frame.

The contact form

When you send the contact form, your name, phone (optional), email (optional), and message go to the shop's inbox and are used only to answer you. They aren't sold, shared, or added to any mailing list.

Questions

Want something removed or have a privacy question? Contact the shop or email neonkittenpdx@gmail.com.
