Cat-Themed Gifts in Vancouver, WA

Looking for a cat gift that isn't boring? Neon Kitten is the cat-gift shop in downtown Vancouver with cattitude — cat-themed gifts and merchandise, filtered through a punk rock sensibility. Cute, but never polite.

The shop was profiled by The Columbian on November 10, 2026 as “a shop with cattitude”: owner Dawn Loehlein merged her love of cats with her punk sensibilities, and the cat lane is where that shows up loudest.

Questions, answered

What kind of cat gifts do you carry?

Cat-themed gifts and merchandise — the shop's tagline is Cats. Punk. Things., and its coverage in The Columbian stresses the unusual end: the weirder, the better. There's no published online catalog, so visit or email the shop about current stock.

Is Neon Kitten a pet store?

It's a retail gift shop — the cats are the theme, not the stock. Google lists Neon Kitten as a store at 621 Broadway St in Vancouver, WA.

Where are you and when are you open?

621 Broadway St, Vancouver, WA 98660, in downtown Vancouver. Wednesday to Saturday 12pm–6pm, Sunday 11am–3pm, closed Monday and Tuesday.

How much are things?

Prices aren't published online. Visit the shop or email neonkittenpdx@gmail.com with the item you have in mind.

Hunting a specific cat gift?

Email the shop with the item in mind, or come browse Broadway — Wednesday to Saturday 12–6, Sunday 11–3.
