Lane 02 · neon kitten

Punk Finds, Vintage & Secondhand in Vancouver, WA

Punk at Neon Kitten isn't decoration — it's the owner's résumé. Dawn Loehlein worked as a manager at Hot Topic for many years before opening her own shop on Broadway, and the shelves follow that history: goods with a stance, vintage with history, oddities with no category at all.

Ask about a find See the cat lane
“…the weirder, the better, like the vintage and secondhand things.” The Columbian · Nov 10, 2026

Secondhand means one of each. The vintage and pre-loved lane is where “the weirder, the better” pays out — pieces that passed through other lives first, the kind you can't order from a catalog.

Questions, answered

What punk items do you sell?

The shop mixes the cat and punk worlds: goods with a punk sensibility, plus vintage and secondhand items. Specific stock isn't listed online, so email neonkittenpdx@gmail.com about what you're hunting for.

Who owns Neon Kitten?

Dawn Loehlein of Vancouver — she calls herself the shop's “meowner” and “opurrator.” She worked as a manager at Hot Topic for many years, and The Columbian profiled the shop's opening on November 10, 2026.

Is it all cats?

No. The tagline is Cats. Punk. Things., and the third word does real work: the shop carries punk goods, vintage and secondhand finds, and outright oddities, not only cat items.

When can I come dig through the vintage?

Wednesday to Saturday 12pm–6pm and Sunday 11am–3pm, closed Monday and Tuesday. The shop is at 621 Broadway St, Vancouver, WA 98660, in downtown Vancouver.

Hunting something specific?

Stock moves and nothing is listed online. Email the shop, or come dig around Broadway.
